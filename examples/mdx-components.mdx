## PromptBlock

When it's done, the new bot shows up in your sidebar. Open it and check that the brief landed:

<PromptBlock
  title="check the brief landed"
  text={`in three lines: what's your job, what do you never do, and when do you stop for me?`}
/>

Without a title, it uses the default:

<PromptBlock text={"Summarise [the document] in five bullets for [your audience]."} />

## Callout

<Callout variant="rule">
  One job per bot. If you're tempted to add a second, make a second bot.
</Callout>

<Callout variant="note">
  **Brief:** the short file that tells a bot what it does, what it never does, and when to stop.
</Callout>

## Steps

<Steps>
- Open the app and choose **New bot**.
- Paste the brief from the prompt above.
- Send the check-in prompt and compare the answer to your brief.
</Steps>

## Checklist

<Checklist title="Before you trust it">
- It names its one job
- It lists what it never does
- It says when it stops and asks
</Checklist>

## Kbd

Press <Kbd>⌘ Space</Kbd>, type the app name, then press <Kbd>Return</Kbd>.
